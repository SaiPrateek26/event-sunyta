The conversation

Why start-ups belong in the allocation conversation

Early-stage companies are no longer just a founder's story or a friend's cheque. For a growing number of investors and advisors, they are a distinct allocation with their own return pattern, access routes and risks. This morning is about understanding that asset class on its own terms.

What we'll explore

Where it fits

How a start-up allocation sits alongside equity, debt and real assets — and how much is too much.

How to access it

Angel investing, funds, syndicates and direct deals — what each route asks of you.

What can go wrong

Illiquidity, failure rates and long holding periods — and how to size the risk honestly.

How to evaluate

Founders, traction, valuation discipline and diligence — a practitioner's checklist.
