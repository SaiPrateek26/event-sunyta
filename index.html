The conversation

Why start-ups belong in the allocation conversation

Early-stage companies are no longer just a founder's story or a friend's cheque. For a growing number of investors and advisors, they are a distinct allocation with their own return pattern, access routes and risks. This day is about understanding that asset class on its own terms.

What we'll explore

Where it fits

How a start-up allocation sits alongside equity, debt and real assets — and how much is too much.

How to access it

Angel investing, funds, syndicates and direct deals — what each route asks of you.

What can go wrong

Illiquidity, failure rates and long holding periods — and how to size the risk honestly.

How to evaluate

Founders, traction, valuation discipline and diligence — a practitioner's checklist.

The idea

Why we built this

Most finance conferences are the same room, repeated: a stage, a sponsor deck, two hundred lanyards, and a hallway of business cards nobody follows up on.

Financial District Circle is our attempt at the opposite. Every edition is small on purpose — a private venue, a hand-picked guest list, and a gathering built around real conversation, not a trade-show floor. A short set of curated sessions, never a sponsor pitch disguised as a panel. Just people who do serious work in financial planning, wealth management and fintech, in one room, talking like peers.

It's built and hosted by the team behind SunYta — the same group behind SunYta Magazine, the CFP Fast Track program, and a community of 300+ Certified Financial Planners. The Circle is where that community meets in person.
